How the work moves

The roadmap is the order you first build things in. After month 12 the same loop keeps running.

01 Find

Discover

Inventory, scans, data maps, vendor lists, pentest results, incidents.

02 Rank

Triage

Each gap becomes a ticket with a severity, an owner and a due date.

03 Fix

Fix, then guard

Fix it, then add a CI check, policy or process so it can't come back.

04 Prove

Verify

Retest, collect evidence for the audit, show customers.

05 Watch

Monitor

Alerts, access reviews, metrics. New signals go back to 01.
